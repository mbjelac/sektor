The planet had 5120 triangles (EDGE_DIVISIONS = 16), of which about 2800 were habitable: 1960 land
triangles outside the polar zones and 859 sea triangles next to land. To get more, smaller areas
while keeping the land-sea ratio (38.8% land), the planet is refined to EDGE_DIVISIONS = 32: 20480
triangles in 96 rings.

The world is not generated anew but refined: every old triangle is split into 4 smaller ones which
inherit what it was (climate, biome, land, forest, desert), so continents, biomes and sektor
geography stay where they were. Split alone would look exactly like the old planet, so borders are
then roughened at the new resolution: triangles on a border are flipped to the other side, as many
one way as the other, so the shares stay the same.

The topology rules of plan 55 still hold with 32 edge divisions: rings 0–31 northern cap
(5 · (2N + 1) triangles), rings 32–63 band (320 triangles each), rings 64–95 southern cap.

The refined planet has 7829 land triangles outside the polar zones and 2861 sea triangles next to
land: 10690 habitable triangles.
