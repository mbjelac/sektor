Current terrain looks ridiculous.
The "blocks" (mountains) are too high and look artificial.

#1 [done] Lower floor block height
- [done] floor blocks with altitude > 0 should be just a bit higher than floor blocks with altitude 0
    - floor block with altitude 1 should be 10% higher than floor block with altitude 0
        - so, 110% of altitude 0 block height
    - floor block with altitude 2 should be 20% higher than floor block with altitude 0
    - floor block with altitude 3 should be 30% higher than floor block with altitude 0
    - etc.

#2 Cliffs should be possible but rare
- maximum altitude should be 9
- a cliff is formed when two neigbouring squares have altitude difference more than 1
- when creating the altitude matrix, almost always set altitudes with a difference of 1
    - start creating mountains from a peak
        - a peak of 9 should be rare
        - choose peak altitude with falling likelihood, like
            - 1 20%
            - 2 20%
            - 3 20%
            - 4 10%
            - 5 10%
            - 6 5%
            - 7 5%
            - 8 5%
            - 9 5%
    - then surround them with blocks lower by 1
    - then surround those with blocks lower by 1
    - repeat until altitude 0 or map edge is reached
    - on a lower likelihood (let's assume 20%) drop elevation by 2 instead of 1
    - on an even lower likelihood (let's assume 10%) drop elevation by 3 instead of 1
