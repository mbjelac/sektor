Buildings are constructed without (visible) cost.
However, it is realistic that this construction also requires resources like functioning of buildings does.

One way to solve this is to keep track of how fast (in real time) buildings are constructed and transform this into resource flow.

Example:
- to keep things simple, let's assume all buildings require 1 steel, 2 concrete and 2 glass
- if, within one sektor, 50 buildings are constructed in 3 days,
    - 50/3 buildings per day roughly 17
    - imports of:
        - steel 17
        - concrete 34
        - glass 34
- these imports are *dynamic*
    - on the day the player built 10 buildings from scratch, nothing shows up in imports
    - on the 2nd day, however, the player sees
        - 10 steel
        - 20 concrete
        - 20 glass
    - if the player builds nothing on the 2nd day, on the 3rd day imports are
        - 5 steel
        - 10 concrete
        - 10 glass
    - after a while (real time) of not building in the sektor, imports drop to 0 or almost 0


EVEN SIMPLER way is to just sum up all resources required for construction, divide each by 10 (or some other constant) and add them to imports.

Example:
- building A uses Glass 5, Concrete 12 & Steal 7
- building B uses Glass 3, Concrete 9 & Steal 9
- after building one A & one B, imports are increased by:
    - Glass 1 ((5 + 3) / 10 = 0.8 ~ 1)
    - Concrete 2 ((12 + 9) / 10 = 2.1 ~ 2)
    - Steal 2 ((7 + 9) / 10 = 1.6 ~ 2)

Explanation: Since import are *flow* of materials, it has to be much lower than the actual amount of materials required.
