Completed mandates bring points to the player.

The stricter the mandate, the higher the score.

In such way a player's score is solely dependant on mandates. No mandate is linked to a specific sektor
- a player may use however many sektors they wish to complete a mandate. However, all claimed mandates have to stay completed to keep their scores.

#1 Mandates have points
- when creating a mandate, also create it's points
- a mandate's points never change
- a mandate point amount depends on the mandate's difficulty to pass
    - examples of VERY difficult mandates:
        - population <= 0
        - pollution <= 0
        - deforestation <= 0
        - export sewage <= 0 (sewage is a negative resource)
    - examples of somewhat difficult mandates:
        - population >= 10000
            - a large number required but given enough sektors, player can achieve this by building enough Habitats
        - pollution <= 100
            - not very strict at start but will limit player's building construction soon
        - export X >= 1000
            - doable but exporting so much will definitely require a lot of imports or at least very large & intricate sektors
    - examples of medium difficulty mandates:
        - population >= 400
            - 10 Habitats will do it
        -
- in mandate list, points are displayed in bottom right of mandate list item panel
    - small
    - prepended by star icon

#2 Completed mandates increase player's score
- player score is visible always in front of player name in top right
    - bold
    - prepended by star icon
- a player's score is the sum of all their completed mandates' points
    - if a player abandons or no longer completes a mandate, their score goes down by that mandate's point amount
    - if a player completes a mandate or claims a mandate they already complete, their score goes up by that mandate's point amount
