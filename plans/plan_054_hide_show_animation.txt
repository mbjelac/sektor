Bodies are hidden for a while by giving them a fully transparent color and toggling it with act.
A dedicated modifier ashow(dt1,dt2[,dt3]) replaces that: the body is hidden for dt1 ms, shown in
its own c() color for dt2 ms, hidden again for dt3 ms (default 0), repeating, in sync across lines
like act. A hidden body is not drawn at all.
