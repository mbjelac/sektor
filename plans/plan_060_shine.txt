Bodies can shine: they glow with their own color, so that faces turned away from the light stay bright,
and glare where the light catches them.
Both architect & frontend use the shared rendering code, so both show shine and its animation.

#1 [done] Objects can shine
- [done] when shine specified, the body glows with its own color and glares where the light catches it
    - shine specification is sh(v)
        - v: shine strength, 0-100
            - 0 is no shine; it is the default when sh is not present
            - 100 adds 60% of the body's own color as glow on top of the light falling on it
                - c(#ffffff) sh(100) is pure white
        - the glare is a specular highlight whose strength follows v

#2 [done] Shine can be animated
- [done] when shine animation specified, the body's shine changes gradually from 0 to the specified strength and back
    - shine animation specification is ash(v, duration, pause)
        - v: shine strength to arrive to, 0-100
        - duration: time in milliseconds during which shine changes from 0 to v
        - pause: time in milliseconds during which shine stays unchanged at each end
    - phases, repeating endlessly:
        - shine increases from 0 to v during duration
        - shine stays at v during pause
        - shine decreases from v back to 0 during duration
        - shine stays at 0 during pause
    - animation is applied in animation steps, like the other animations (plan 13)
