Existing mandate criteria are import & export. These are "economic" mandates.

Other categories & criteria:
- ecological
    - deforestation
    - pollution
- social
    - population density
    - hapiness

Deforestation:
Current ecosystem support damage can be transformed to deforestation.
deforestation = initial EcosystemSupport - current EcosystemSupport
deforestationPercentage = 100 * (initial EcosystemSupport - current EcosystemSupport) / initial EcosystemSupport
Total player's deforestation across all player's sektors can be calculated as:
deforestationPercentageTotal = 100 *  sum(initial EcosystemSupport - current EcosystemSupport) / sum(initial EcosystemSupport))

Pollution:
A sektor's pollution metric is a value based on number of buildings (including forests) affected by pollution on their square and the percentage of the pollution.
An empty square with pollution does not add to the pollution metric.
Affected: Squares with forests or other buildings affected by pollution (buildings with groundwater & soil inputs, habitats, etc.)
Pollution metric: amount of the square's pollution percentage.
Example:
A sektor has:
- 2 empty squares with pollutions 40% & 80%
- 1 forest with pollutions 20%
- 1 WaterWells with pollution 20%
- 2 Habitats with pollution 20% & 60%
Total sektor pollution: 120
Total player's pollution metric is then the sum of all player's sektors pollutions.
