In order to achieve a sense of progress in the game, players can claim Mandates.
A mandate is a set of criteria.
A mandate is completed when all player's sektors fullfills the criteria.
A mandate is *again* incomplete when all player's sektors *no longer* fullfills the criteria.
Completed mandates bring points to the player.

Examples:
- mandate 1
    - imports of A, B & C must be under 50 in total
    - imports of D must be 0
    - exports of E must be at least 20
- mandate 2
    - population must be at least 60
- mandate 3
    - hapiness must be at least 70%
- mandate 4
    - ecological damage must be under 50%

The stricter the mandate, the higher the score.

In such way a player's score is solely dependant on mandates. No mandate is linked to a specific sektor
- a player may use however many sektors they wish to fulfill a mandate. However, all claimed mandates have to stay fulfilled to keep their scores.

    - strategy is chosen at random: 8/10 positive exports & imports, 1/10 negative imports, 1/10 negative exports
    - minimum positive exports & maximum positive imports
        - choose an export & an amount as a minimum export requirement
        - choose several imports and amounts for maximum import requirements
            - 1 to 3 imports, how many chosen at random (never more than there are inputs to choose from)
            - inputs with the largest amounts are restricted first
            - choose imports to restrict by following algorithm:
                - find building definitions which output the chosen export
                - choose imports from their inputs
        - choose amounts of exports & imports to be balanced
            - avoid chosing requiring very low exports & very high imports, and vice versa
                - try to balance using the building function
                - maximum import = minimum export × (input per unit of output in the building function) × random factor between 0.5 and 1 (exclusive), kept within the range of amount values
                - maximum import is always less than what the minimum export needs, so some local production of that input is needed
                    - an input of which the minimum export needs no more than the lowest amount value is not restricted
    - minimum negative imports
        - choose a negative resource
        - choose an amount
        - add a "minimum import" criteria for that resource & amount
    - maximum negative exports
        - choose a negative resource
        - choose an amount
        - add a "maximum export" criteria for that resource & amount
    - amount values should be chosen randomly from a wide range
        - lowest value 5
        - highest value 50
