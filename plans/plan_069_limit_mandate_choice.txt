A player's score is the number of their claimed mandates which are currently fulfilled.
For this to measure progress, a player must not be able to collect mandates without working for them.

Currently many generated mandates are already fulfilled when they appear, for example:
- population at most (habitats + 5) * 40
- pollution at most the player's own pollution footprint
- deforestation at most 20 forests
- hapiness at least 1
Since mandates are refilled every second and any number can be claimed, a player could collect dozens of these without building anything.

Unclaimed mandates are kept in a shared pool:
- the pool always has unclaimed mandates (see #2)
- the pool size does not depend on the number of players
    - each player sees only a part of the pool (see below), and which part depends on the player's state, not on how many players there are
    - claimed mandates are replaced right away, so many players claiming do not shrink the pool
- the pool is shared by all players, so a mandate is not created for any particular player (see #1)

Which mandates a player sees:
- "at least" mandates are shown only while the player does not already fulfil them (see #3)
- "at most" mandates are shown whether or not the player already fulfils them
    - a player can take on a limit in advance, as a commitment which will constrain them later
    - a point now, at the cost of flexibility later
    - or a player can take on a limit they already break, and work to fix the situation

Which mandates a player can claim:
- a mandate the player cannot claim is still shown
    - Claim is not possible
    - reason shown, so the player can decide to abandon something to make room
- a player holds at most one mandate per criterion (see #5)
- a player holds at most 3 unfulfilled mandates (see #4)
