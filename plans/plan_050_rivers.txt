A river is a series of squares connected to each other linearly starting at one edge of the map and ending in another edge of the map.
A river square may not have a building.

Transport: Edges of a river square may have a bycicle bridge over it. Same types apply as for bicycle paths.

Special case, a river may fork.

Different river sizes - some rivers are narrow, some are wide.

Water is animated.

#1 A sektor may have a river flowing through it
- when creating a sektor, first create ground (0) and sea (1), then *before* elevation create a river
    - a sektor may have one river, with 50% probability
    - terrain matrix values for river are 3, 4, 5 & 6
    - a river should be generated from an *edge* of the map (not a sea coast!)
    - then meander throught the map by flowing to the neighbouring square - sraight, left or right
    - circling back in on itself is not allowed!
    - when reaching a square on the sea coast, stop
    - when reaching another map edge, stop
    - use a value from 3 to 6 depending on the source of the river for that square:
        - use one number for each direction from which the river has flowed into the square
        - example:
                .3....
                .344..
                ...3..
                .553..
                .3....
            - the river started in the top-left corner of the map
            - 3 is the value meaning river had flowed in from north
            - then it takes a turn: 4 is the value meaning river had flowed in from west
            - then turns again to south (3)
            - then turns to west: 5 is the value meaning river had flowed in from east
            - not in the example, but if the river had turned towards north we would use 6
            - values are just for example, but whatever value chosen for whatever direction, this should be consistent for all generated rivers
- proceed to create elevations on ground only (0)
- render river as a blue strip going across the floor
