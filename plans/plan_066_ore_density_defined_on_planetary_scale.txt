Ore density is defined on planetary scale.
Areas where a type of ore is high in denisty span more than one sektor.

For example, metal ore seams span 3-4 connected areas (regardless of climate zone, biome type or land/sea) which makes metal ore of high density & amount in every sektor in those areas.

#1 [done] Planet's areas are given high-ore-density marks
- [done] mark: ore name
    - one of: metals, uranium, minerals
- [done] an area cannot have multiple marks
- [done] generate marks based on algorithm:
    - metals: "draw" curves on globe by marking neighbouring areas as metal-rich
        - a curve is a sequence of neighbouring areas
        - length 2 to 5 areas long, randomly
        - they are not linear, but have a crecent shape
        - 70 curves globally
        - 20% of curves along coastlines
        - 40% inland
        - 40% seabed
    - minerals: curvy, slightly broken or straight lines
        - length 2 to 4 areas long
        - 80 lines globally
        - random distribution across the globe
    - uranium: clusters of neighbouring areas with empty areas in between
        - a cluster may have 1 to 4 areas randomly
        - 30 clusters globally
        - random distribution across the globe
- [done] when generating, generate first metals, then minerals, then uranium
    - if area already marked, replace old mark with new one
        - minerals replace metals, uranium replaces both minerals & metals
