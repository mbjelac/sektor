Ore density is defined on planetary scale.
Areas where a type of ore is high in denisty span more than one sektor.

For example, metal ore seams span 3-4 connected areas (regardless of climate zone, biome type or land/sea) which makes metal ore of high density & amount in every sektor in those areas.

#1 [done] Planet's areas are given high-ore-density marks
- [done] mark: ore name
    - one of: metals, uranium, minerals
- [done] an area cannot have multiple marks
- [done] generate marks based on algorithm:
    - metals: "draw" curves on globe by marking neighbouring areas as metal-rich
        - a curve is a sequence of neighbouring areas
        - length 2 to 5 areas long, randomly
        - they are not linear, but have a crecent shape
        - 70 curves globally
        - 20% of curves along coastlines
        - 40% inland
        - 40% seabed
    - minerals: curvy, slightly broken or straight lines
        - length 2 to 4 areas long
        - 80 lines globally
        - random distribution across the globe
    - uranium: clusters of neighbouring areas with empty areas in between
        - a cluster may have 1 to 4 areas randomly
        - 30 clusters globally
        - random distribution across the globe
- [done] when generating, generate first metals, then minerals, then uranium
    - if area already marked, replace old mark with new one
        - minerals replace metals, uranium replaces both minerals & metals
- [done] player can see ore density on globe
    - in list view, show on globe which area has which high ore density
        - display this as a filled circle in the color of the location property (metals, uranium, minerals)
        - display circle in center of area triangle, diameter 2 pixels smaller then width of sektor square mark
        - on area mouse over, display ore type in bottom data row
            - after biome, with same spacing as between climate zone & biome
            - display: "<swatch> <ore name>"

#2 [done] A portion of sektors are on ore-rich areas
- [done] when generating a new sektor, there is a 1 in 4 chance it is on an ore-rich area
- [done] if sektor is on an ore-rich area, pick a location for a sektor as always, but filter only ore-rich areas for picking
- [done] if sektor is not on an ore-rich area, pick a location for a sektor as always, but filter only ore-poor areas for picking

#3 Area rich in ore type has abundance of that ore in it's locations
- use existing algorithms for generating sektor location property matrices
- if area is not marked as rich with that ore
    - maximum amount of that ore in sektor is 2
    - interpolate rest of values according to existing algorithm, rounded to integer values
- if area is marked as rich with that ore
    - maximum amount of that ore in sektor is 12
