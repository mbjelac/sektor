Each building, when constructed, plays a little "construction animation" - construction rig, various things going up and expanding, etc.
A little detail, contribution to gameplay is zero, but it might significantly boost the atmosphere.

Technical: The construction animation can be thought of as another building, with animations after which it ends up looking like the constructed building.
All animations should last for max 4-5 seconds after which the animation buildings dissapears and the actual building is shown.

#1 Player sees the building being constructed
- when a building is placed on the map, do not render it immediately
    - first render from the building specification "Construction Render" section
    - the section should have a "duration" property which has a value in milliseconds
        - example: "duration=1500" is 1500ms, or 1 and a half second
    - after this duration passes, remove the Construction Render rendering and replace it with the building rendering (from the Render section)
- if a building specification does not have the Construction Render section, place the building immediately as before
- in test mode do NOT use contruction render
