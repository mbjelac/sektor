Various visual styles for empty squares:
- temperate (started with this; european-style hills, mountains, forests, lakes, sparse simple rivers)
- tropical (jungles, lots of water & complex river systems, jungle-covered mountains)
- polar/subpolar (snow, ice, evergeen forests, icy rocky gray mountains, snow animation!)
- desert (sand, no water, sparse bushes & cactuses, rocky mountains & stone features)

Some location properties very much depend on biome:

| Biome                    | Soil | Groundwater | Wind | Insolation |
|--------------------------|------|-------------|------|------------|
| 🌴 Tropical rainforest   | 1    | 5           | 1    | 4          |
| 🌿 Savanna               | 3    | 3           | 4    | 5          |
| 🌾 Grassland             | 5    | 3           | 4    | 4          |
| 🏜️ Desert                | 0    | 0           | 5    | 5          |
| 🌳 Temperate forest      | 4    | 4           | 3    | 3          |
| 🌲 Taiga / boreal forest | 1    | 3           | 4    | 1          |
| ❄️ Tundra                | 1    | 1           | 5    | 1          |
| 🌊 Freshwater            | 3    | 5           | 3    | 3          |
| 🌊 Marine                | 0    | 4           | 5    | 3          |

Biome structure:
temperate
    forest
    grassland
    marine
tropical
    forest (jungle)
    grassland (savanna)
    marine
polar
    forest (taiga)
    grassland (tundra)
    marine
desert

So, except for the Desert, all biome groups have sub-groups:
- grassland: very little trees (and very sparesly wooded elevations)
- forest: contains forests (buildings which can be cleared for negative points)
- marine: like grassland, but mostly sea

#7 Grasslands have very little forest
- when generating forests, take biome sub-type into account:
    - forest sub-type (land area marked with F) has at random 50%-90% forests
    - grassland sub-type (land area not marked with F) has at random 0%-15% forests
    - sea (area not marked with L) has at random 0%-10% forests

#8 Biomes affect insolation
- when generating insolation matrix, take biomes into account:
    - desert: all matrix elements are the same number
        - tropical: 12
        - temperate: 8
        - polar: 5
    - forest: create randomly 2-6 "pockets" of high insolation throughout the sektor
        - pockets have a high point and then fall off as the distance from that point grows
        - high point values per biome type:
            - tropical: 8
            - temperate: 6
            - polar: 2
    - grassland: create randomly 2-6 "pockets" of high insolation throughout the sektor
        - pockets have a high point and then fall off as the distance from that point grows
        - high point values per biome type:
            - tropical: 10
            - temperate: 8
            - polar: 3
