A player's score is the number of their claimed mandates which are currently completed (see plan_limit_mandate_choice).
Completing mandates allows the player to own more sektors: a new player may own few sektors, the allowance grows as the score grows.

Number of sektors & mandates balance each other:
- all player's sektors count towards all player's mandates
- more sektors make "at least" mandates easier
    - more space & resources for exports, population, hapiness
- more sektors make "at most" mandates harder
    - more buildings polluting, more forests felled
- so a larger allowance is not simply an advantage, it is a choice the player has to manage

Sektor allowance depends directly on number of completed mandates:
- no levels (see plan_limit_building_choice)
- a player cannot claim a sektor if they already own as many sektors as their allowance permits
- a new player's allowance must be enough to complete the first mandates

Losing completed mandates:
- completion is live, so the score can drop and with it the allowance
- sektors already owned are kept
- the player cannot claim new sektors until they are back within the allowance
- the player keeps what they have, but cannot expand
