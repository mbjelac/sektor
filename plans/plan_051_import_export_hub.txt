Import & export (in & out of a sektor) is not automatically possible.
Shipping stuff in & out requires a special building - an inter-city transport hub.

If the building does not exist:
- when imports & exports are calculated
- mark buildings and recalculate imports without marked buildings until import amount is 0
- exports are cropped to 0 (nothing can be shipped out)
- marked buildings should be indicated to the player on the map, and the reason should be visible in the building panel

Capacity:
There can be multiple import/export hubs. Each hub increases the import capacity and the export capacity of the sektor.
Import capacity and export capacity are the same - the total sum of ImportExport outputs.
Imports and exports are limited separately:
- imports: when buildings marked on each building placement or destruction, if total amount of imports is less or equal to import capacity, no buildings are marked.
  Mark buildings and recalculate until this is the case. Exports have no say in marking buildings.
  Start marking buildings farthest from the closest import/export hub.
- exports: no buildings are marked. If total amount of exports is bigger than export capacity, exports are cropped to export capacity - excess exports are just wasted.
