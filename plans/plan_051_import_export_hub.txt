Import & export (in & out of a sektor) is not automatically possible.
Shipping stuff in & out requires a special building - an inter-city transport hub.

If the building does not exist:
- when imports & exports are calculated
- mark buildings and recalculate imports & exports without marked buildings until import amount is 0
- marked buildings should be indicated to the player on the map, and the reason should be visible in the building panel

Capacity:
There can be multiple import/export hubs. Each hub increases the imports/exports capacity of the sektor.
When buildings marked on each building placement or destruction, if total number of imports & exports is less or equal to total import/export capacity, no buildings are marked.
Mark buildings and recalculate until this is the case.
Start marking buildings farthest from the closest import/export hub.

#1 Sektor imports & exports are limited by hub capacity
- when imports & exports are calculated
    - sum up the total ImportExport resources = "capacity"
    - sum up the total amount of imports and exports = "amount"
        - ImportExport is local so it doesn't show up in exports
    - amount must be less or equal to capacity
    - hubs are buildings which have ImportExport as output
    - if amount is greater than capacity
        - order buildings by biggest distance from nearest hub
        - mark building as disabled
        - recalculate imports & exports without including marked buildings
    - repeat until amount not greater than capacity
- disabled buildings do not consume inputs nor produce outputs
- disabled buildings are marked on map by floating warning
- disabled building panel marked
    - contains warning
        - text: "⚠️ Building missing required inputs!"
        - white text
        - below building label
    - all input & output amounts displayed as "0 / amount"
- player is notified with a warning message
    - text: "Some buildings cannot function: Missing required inputs!"
- player is notified with a warning message
    - text: "Import & export capacity exceeded! Optimize sektor or build more transport hubs."
