Import & export (in & out of a sektor) is not automatically possible.
Shipping stuff in & out requires a special building - an inter-city transport hub.

If the building does not exist:
- when imports & exports are calculated
- mark buildings and recalculate imports without marked buildings until import amount is 0
- exports are cropped to 0 (nothing can be shipped out)
- marked buildings should be indicated to the player on the map, and the reason should be visible in the building panel

Capacity:
There can be multiple import/export hubs. Each hub increases the import capacity and the export capacity of the sektor.
Import capacity and export capacity are the same - the total sum of ImportExport outputs.
Imports and exports are limited separately:
- imports: when buildings marked on each building placement or destruction, if total amount of imports is less or equal to import capacity, no buildings are marked.
  Mark buildings and recalculate until this is the case. Exports have no say in marking buildings.
  Start marking buildings farthest from the closest import/export hub.
- exports: no buildings are marked. If total amount of exports is bigger than export capacity, exports are cropped to export capacity - excess exports are just wasted.

#1 [done] Sektor imports & exports are limited by hub capacity
- [done] when imports & exports are calculated
    - sum up the total ImportExport resources = "capacity"
        - import capacity and export capacity are both equal to it
    - sum up the total amount of imports = "import amount"
        - ImportExport is local so it doesn't show up in exports
    - import amount must be less or equal to import capacity
    - hubs are buildings which have ImportExport as output
    - if import amount is greater than import capacity
        - order buildings by biggest distance from nearest hub
        - mark building as disabled
        - recalculate imports & exports without including marked buildings
    - repeat until import amount not greater than import capacity
    - exports are not considered when disabling buildings
- [done] exports are cropped to export capacity
    - exports are cropped after buildings are disabled, from the exports of the remaining buildings
    - sum up the total amount of exports = "export amount"
    - if export amount is not greater than export capacity, exports are left as they are
    - if export amount is greater than export capacity
        - excess = export amount - export capacity
        - crop exports: take an equal amount ("crop amount") from every export, so that the cropped exports add up to export capacity
        - an export cannot go below 0
            - an export smaller than the crop amount is cropped to 0
            - what it could not give up is taken equally from the remaining exports, which raises the crop amount
        - finding the crop amount:
            - go through exports with amount above 0, from smallest to biggest
            - if all remaining exports can give up the rest of the excess before the current (smallest) one reaches 0
                - divide the rest of the excess equally among them - done
            - otherwise
                - all remaining exports give up as much as the current one has left
                - subtract that from the excess and continue with the next export
        - cropped export amounts are rounded down to one decimal, so their sum never goes above export capacity
        - no buildings are disabled, excess exports are wasted
        - example: export capacity 4, exports Stone 6 and Water 1 (export amount 7, excess 3)
            - Water gives up its 1, Stone gives up 1 - excess left 1
            - only Stone remains, it gives up the last 1
            - result: Stone 4, Water 0
    - "cropped exports" are the exports whose amount was reduced by cropping
        - their number is the X in the export warning message
    - the sektor state lists the cropped exports by resource name
