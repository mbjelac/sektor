    - scoring is done per following rules:
        - each imported resource has score -2 per unit
            - importing 17 of a resource has a score of -34
        - each exported resource has score +2 per unit
            - exporting 17 of a resource has a score of 34
        - if a resource is required (has export requirement), required amount is scored +3, amount above requirement is scored regularly
            - exporting 12 of a resource with requirement 5 has a score of 29
                - required 5 * 3 = 15
                - above required 7 * 2 = 14
        - if a resource has negative scoring, flip score values
            - imports are scored +2
            - exports are scored -2
            - a resource has negative scoring if the resource table in resources.md has "negative" word in that resource's line
