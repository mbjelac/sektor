#1 Player sees the leaderboard
- on the sektor list page there is a leaderboard
    - sektor list is on the left half of the screen
    - leaderboard on the right
    - leaderboard is a list with all players (owners of sektors)
    - each list item has:
        - player name
        - total player score (sum of all their sektor scores)
        - level of player
            - computed from player score using the function level=max(1,floor(log2(score+1)))
