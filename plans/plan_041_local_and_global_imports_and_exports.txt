Global player scoring:
- the planet has global imports & exports which are just total sums of all existing sektor imports & exports
    - same as sektor with sum of building inputs & outputs
    - example:
        - sektor A exports water 10 and imports food 15
        - sektor B exports food 8 and imports water 9
        - planet has
            - water export of 1
            - food import of 7
- each player's contribution to exports & imports are scored, taken from every player's sektor
- sektor status is not needed
    - all sektors are active in global imports & exports as soon as the first building is built!
- player scores are dynamic
    - changing all the time
    - each sektor's imports & exports are contributing with positive and negative points to player's score
    - each resource is evaluated for all cases and score applied:
        - importing exported 0 (no damage done)
        - importing imported -1 * global import (worsening the planet's situation)
        - exporting exported +1 * global export (contributing to planet's exports)
        - exporting imported +2 * global import (helping the planet's situation)
    - as a building is built, sektor's imports & exports change, changing global imports & exports & changing all the players' scores
- this empowers the player to build in each sektor towards one, more or none of the current planet's goals!
    - playing the game is not limited by score advancement
    - score advancement is incentivised by the leaderboard (fame!) but nothing else

#2 Sektor's imports & exports represent a "current score"
- previous calculations of score based on imports & exports are to be completely overriden by this new one
- current sektor's imports & exports represent a "current score" of the sektor
- current score is calculated based on current planet's imports and exports as follows:
    - for each resource which is imported or exported to/from the sektor:
        - look up the global import/export status of that resource
        - the current score for that resource is:
            - importing globally exported: -1
            - importing globally imported: -2
            - exporting globally exported: +1
            - exporting globally imported: +2
        - example:
            - sektor is importing Wheat which is globally exported (-1)
            - sektor is exporting Water which is globally exported (1)
            - sektor is importing Food which is globally imported (-2)
            - resulting current score: -2
- display current scores of each resource in sektor map import/export panel
    - already implemented in UI, apply to current score for each resource
- display total current score in sektor map import/export panel
    - already implemented in UI, apply to total current score
- changing amount of imports or exports here changes amount of global imports or exports shown on the list import/export panel
- import/export panel title: Imports & Exports, Local
    - left-aligned
