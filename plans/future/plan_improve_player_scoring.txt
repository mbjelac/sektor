Abandon scoring imports & exports, including required exports, negative resources, etc.

Scoring should be a mechanism for pushing a player to behave in a certain way.
For example, instead of just plopping buildings, player has to investigate whether some buildings can do multiple work by turning on more functions (if the building has that feature).

Player score is tied to a difficulty level:
A player cannot jump from level 1 to level 5 by just playing a bunch of sektors on level 1.
All sektors played on level 1 only contribute to player reaching level 2.
All sektors on level 2 contribute just for level 3, etc.

# Construction efficiency contributes to player score
- each Done Sektor adds to player score with the construction efficiency score
- construction efficiency is the number of empty squares on a Done sektor (how *little* buildings player constructed to pass requirements & restrictions)
- each empty square equals X contruction efficiency points

# Happiness contributes to player score
- each Done Sektor adds to player score with the happiness score
- happiness score is the total sum of Happiness resource output by all Habitats in the sektor

# Player sees construction efficiency on sektor map
- next to stats

# Player sees happiness on sektor map
- next to stats

# Player sees their percentage for reaching next level
- in leaderboard
