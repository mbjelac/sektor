A player's score is the number of their claimed mandates which are currently completed (see plan_limit_mandate_choice).
Completing mandates gives the player a richer building palette: a new player has few building types, more are unlocked as the score grows.

Levels are no longer needed:
- currently score is turned into a level (levels.md), and a building can require a level (minLevel)
- the score is a small, readable number (count of completed mandates), so buildings can require it directly
    - one indirection less: no table to keep in sync with the buildings
    - the unlocking curve is tuned directly in buildings.md
- no building in buildings.md uses minLevel yet, so nothing is lost by removing it
- remove:
    - levels.md & levels.test.md
    - parseLevels.ts & its tests
    - playerLevel.ts
    - minLevel building property

Buildings require a number of completed mandates:
- a building may have a property with the minimum number of completed mandates the player needs to build it
    - replaces minLevel
    - a building without this property is available from the start
- the building toolbar offers only buildings the player has enough completed mandates for
    - same as currently done with levels
    - destruction tool always offered
- the starting set of buildings must be enough to complete the first mandates

Losing completed mandates:
- completion is live, so the score can drop and buildings can become locked again
- buildings already constructed stay & keep working
- locked buildings cannot be constructed again until the player has enough completed mandates
    - no change needed: toolbar filtering already works so
- the player keeps what they have, but cannot expand with locked buildings

Leaderboard:
- remove "Level" & "Points to next level" columns
- possibly replace with number of completed mandates needed for the next building unlock
