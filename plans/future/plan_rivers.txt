A river is a series of squares connected to each other linearly starting at one edge square and ending in another edge square not on the same edge.
A river square may not have a building (maybe a Riverloop, or some hydroelectric plant?).

Transport: Edges of a river square may have a bycicle bridge over it. Same types apply as for bicycle paths.

Special case, a river may fork.

Different river sizes - some rivers are narrow, some are wide.

Water is animated.
