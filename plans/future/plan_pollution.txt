Some buildings emit air, soild or water pollution.
This is not part of imports & exports but it does have an area of effect.
If other buildings are constructed within that area they are effected.
WaterWells affected by water pollution: they produce WaterTechnical instead of WaterPottable.
Habitats affected by air pollution: their HealthPhysical is down to 0 or reduced (also can depend on how close to the building they are)
