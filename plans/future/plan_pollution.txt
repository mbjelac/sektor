Some buildings emit air, soil or water pollution.
This is not part of imports & exports but it does have an area of effect.
If other buildings are constructed within that area they are affected.

Table of buildings and which pollution(s) they are affected by and in what way:
| Building    | Pollution        | Effect                                          |
|-------------|------------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| WaterWells  | water            | disabled                                        |
| Habitats    | air, water       | decreased output of WorkUnqualified, Hapiness   |
| Agriplot    | soil             | disabled                                        |
| Library     | air              | decreased HealthMental                          |
| SunCatcher  | air              | "insolation" property decreases (air particles) |
| Polytechnic | air              | decreased output WorkTechnical                  |
| ChildKeep   | air              | decreased output Childcare                      |
| Gymnasium   | air              | decreased output HealthPhysical                 |

| Park        | air, water, soil | decreased all HealthX outputs                   |
| TownSquare  | air              | decreased output HealthSocial                   |
| Forest      | air, water, soil | decreased all outputs                           |
