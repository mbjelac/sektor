Player score affects:
- which mandates are available to claim
    - high-score players cannot claim low-score mandates
    - low-score players cannot claim high-score mandates
- which buildings are available to the player
    - low score = fewer buildings
- which biomes are available to the player
    - low score = fewer biomes
- how many sektors a player is allowed to own
    - low score = fewer sektors
