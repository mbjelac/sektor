Current problems:
- requirements & restrictions seem arbitrary
- restrictions force the player what to build instead of pushing them with import penalties
- in each sektor, player first looks at requirements and then builds backwards from it
    - repetitive, mundane

Solution sketch:
- replace requirements & restrictions with global player scoring:
    - the planet has global imports & exports which are just total sums of all existing sektor imports & exports
        - same as sektor with sum of building inputs & outputs
        - example:
            - sektor A exports water 10 and imports food 15
            - sektor B exports food 8 and imports water 9
            - planet has
                - water export of 1
                - food import of 7
    - each player's contribution to exports & imports are scored, taken from every player's sektor
    - sektor status is not needed
        - all sektors are active in global imports & exports as soon as the first building is built!
    - player scores are dynamic
        - changing all the time
        - each sektor's imports & exports are contributing with positive and negative points to player's score
        - each resource is evaluated for all cases and score applied:
            - importing exported 0 (no damage done)
            - importing imported -1 * global import (worsening the planet's situation)
            - exporting exported +1 * global export (contributing to planet's exports)
            - exporting imported +2 * global import (helping the planet's situation)
        - as a building is built, sektor's imports & exports change, changing global imports & exports & changing all the players' scores
    - this empowers the player to build in each sektor towards one, more or none of the current planet's goals!
        - playing the game is not limited by score advancement
        - score advancement is incentivised by the leaderboard (fame!) but nothing else

#1 Remove requirements, restrictions & sektor status
- remove from Sektor logic
- remove status display from sektor map stats & sektor list
- remove highlights of imports and exports in import/export panel dependent on meeting requirements and/or restrictions
- remove requirements & restrictions column in sektor map import/export panel
- remove creation algorithm calculations for requirements & restrictions
    - remove solution paths, playtests, walks, etc.

#2 Player can see planetary imports & exports
- for each sektor collect all imports & exports and sum them in global imports & export per resource type
- on list page display a panel with imports & exports of all sektors
    - all sektors regardless of ownership, even of sektors without an owner
    - display import/export panel similar to the one in sektor map
        - without score column

musings:

- this makes the planet similar to a collaborative sandbox where each player's action affects everything else (like it is on the real planet we live on)
- possible expansion of the game is to have more planets (would be cool that they have same buildings, but different floor color scheme, different location property algorithms, etc.)
    - all planets in the game can swap each other's imports & exports!
