A player's score is the number of their claimed mandates which are currently fulfilled (see plan_limit_mandate_choice).
Fulfilling mandates allows the player to claim sektors of more biome types: a new player may claim sektors of few biome types, more are unlocked as the score grows.

Biome types are those shown in sektor list biome column:
- 🌾 Grassland
- 🌳 Forest
- 🏜 Desert
- 🌿 Savanna
- 🌴 Jungle
- 🌱 Tundra
- 🌲 Taiga
- ❄️ Polar
- 🌊 Marine

Biome types require a number of fulfilled mandates:
- each biome type may have a minimum number of fulfilled mandates the player needs to claim a sektor of that biome type
    - a biome type without it is available from the start
    - no levels (see plan_limit_building_choice)
- starting biome types should be the easiest to work with, e.g. Grassland & Forest
    - must be enough to fulfill the first mandates
- harder biome types unlocked later, e.g. Desert, Polar, Marine

Sektors of all biome types are still created:
- sektors are shared by all players, so a sektor is not created for any particular player
- a player may see sektors of biome types they have not unlocked
    - seeing them gives the player a goal
    - they cannot be claimed
        - wherever a sektor can be claimed, Claim is disabled
        - player sees how many fulfilled mandates are needed to claim it

Losing fulfilled mandates:
- completion is live, so the score can drop and biome types can become locked again
- sektors already owned in a locked biome type are kept
- the player cannot claim new sektors of a locked biome type until they have enough fulfilled mandates
- the player keeps what they have, but cannot expand into locked biome types
