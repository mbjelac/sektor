A player's score is the number of their claimed mandates which are currently fulfilled.
For this to measure progress, a player must not be able to collect mandates without working for them.

Currently many generated mandates are already fulfilled when they appear, for example:
- population at most (habitats + 5) * 40
- pollution at most the player's own pollution footprint
- deforestation at most 20 forests
- hapiness at least 1
Since mandates are refilled every second and any number can be claimed, a player could collect dozens of these without building anything.

Mandates are kept in a shared pool:
- the pool always has 100 unclaimed mandates
    - whenever a mandate is claimed, a new one is created to replace it
- the pool size does not depend on the number of players
    - each player sees only a part of the pool (see below), and which part depends on the player's state, not on how many players there are
    - claimed mandates are replaced right away, so many players claiming do not shrink the pool
- the pool is shared by all players, so a mandate is not created for any particular player
    - mandate amounts are drawn from wide ranges valid for the whole planet, not from one player's state
        - import & export amounts already work so, based on planet's imports & exports
        - pollution, deforestation, population & hapiness amounts need planet-wide ranges too
    - the wide ranges mean the pool has mandates for players of all states of development
        - "at least" mandates which a new player can reach
        - "at most" limits which ask a developed player to clean up: destroy polluting buildings, replant forests, remove habitats

No mandate is shown to a player which the player already fulfils:
- each player sees only those mandates in the pool which their sektors do not already fulfil
    - checked against the player's current totals
- a player with nothing to limit sees no ecology mandates
    - fits the game: a new player builds up an economy first, ecology mandates appear once there is damage to limit
- a mandate shown as unfulfilled may become fulfilled before it is claimed (the player changed something meanwhile)
    - that is fine, the player did the work

A player can hold at most 3 unfulfilled mandates:
- fulfilled mandates do not count towards the cap
    - a fulfilled mandate frees its slot, so the player can keep claiming and their score keeps growing
    - capping all held mandates would cap the score, blocking progression
- since every mandate shown starts unfulfilled, the player works on at most 3 mandates at once
- a fulfilled mandate can become unfulfilled again (completion is live) and take up a slot
    - if this puts the player over the cap, nothing is force-abandoned
    - new claims are blocked until the player is back under the cap, by re-fulfilling or abandoning mandates
