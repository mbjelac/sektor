Current terrain looks ridiculous.
The "blocks" (mountains) are too high and look artificial.
One altitude point difference should raise the floor just a bit, maybe 1/10th of the height of the floor block.

The MAX_ALTITUDE should not be a constant at all - since almost all changes in elevation should be continuous (neighbouring floors lower or higher by just 1)
maximum altitude is dependant on sektor size.
Example: In a 6x6 sektor, maximum altitude is 5
    - in the extreme on one edge there is a floor with altitude 0
    - then continued elevation for 5 floors towards the other edge
     - 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 to the highest peak in the sektor

Cliffs (neigbour square altitude differences > 1) should be possible but rare.
