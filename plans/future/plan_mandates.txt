In order to achieve a sense of progress in the game, players can claim Mandates.
A mandate is a set of criteria.
A mandate is completed when all player's sektors fullfills the criteria.
A mandate is *again* incomplete when all player's sektors *no longer* fullfills the criteria.
Completed mandates bring points to the player.

Examples:
- mandate 1
    - imports of A, B & C must be under 50 in total
    - imports of D must be 0
    - exports of E must be at least 20
- mandate 2
    - population must be at least 60
- mandate 3
    - hapiness must be at least 70%
- mandate 4
    - ecological damage must be under 50%

The stricter the mandate, the higher the score.

In such way a player's score is solely dependant on mandates. No mandate is linked to a specific sektor
- a player may use however many sektors they wish to fulfill a mandate. However, all claimed mandates have to stay fulfilled to keep their scores.

Optional: Claiming a mandate brings points in itself. Abandoning a mandate brings negative points.

Player score affects:
- which mandates are available to claim
    - high-score players cannot claim low-score mandates
    - low-score players cannot claim high-score mandates
- which buildings are available to the player
    - low score = fewer buildings
- which biomes are available to the player
    - low score = fewer biomes
- how many sektors a player is allowed to claim
    - low score = fewer sektors
