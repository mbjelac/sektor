In order to achieve a sense of progress in the game, players can claim assignments.
An assignment is a set of criteria.
An assignment is complete when a player's sektor or any combination of player's sektors fullfills the assignment criteria.

Examples:
- assignment X
    - imports of A, B & C must be under 50 in total
    - imports of D must be 0
    - exports of E must be at least 20
