A player may claim a mandate which contradicts a mandate they already hold.
This is allowed on purpose: it lets the player pivot their direction by abandoning an existing mandate and claiming a contradictory one.

A pivot is a choice only if the player sees the conflict before claiming.
Otherwise the player claims a mandate, later discovers it cannot be completed together with the ones they hold, and feels cheated instead of having chosen.

Directly conflicting mandates:
- criteria of the same type which cannot both be completed
- examples:
    - unclaimed "population at most 200" conflicts with held "population at least 400"
    - unclaimed "export at most 10 Metals" conflicts with held "export at least 30 Metals"

Indirectly conflicting mandates:
- held (claimed or completed) mandates which do not directly conflict, but become difficult to work with if the selected mandate is claimed
- examples:
    - unclaimed "pollution at most 50" makes held "export at least 40 Metals" hard, as metal production pollutes
    - unclaimed "deforestation at most 0" makes held "population at least 400" hard, as habitats need space

Idea:
- in an unclaimed mandate's details popup, indicate held mandates which conflict with it
    - e.g. "Conflicts with <mandate icon> 12"
- distinguish direct conflicts (cannot be completed together) from indirect ones (difficult together)
- possibly show multiple conflicting mandates
