To give the player a sense of achievement, badges are awarded when certain conditions are met.
The badge is stored on the player.
If conditions are no longer met, the player does not lose the badge.
If conditions are met again, the player does not gain the badge again.

Ideas for achievements:
- 80% happiness (then 90%, 100%)
- 80-20 export-import ratio
- reducing a global import by 10% (20%, 30%, ...)
- reaching a certain number of exports (or imports, or hapiness) with a very few buildings constructed
- in forest biomes, keeping deforestation a minimum (20%, 10%, 5%, 0%) while having imports & exports

deforestation: Ecologist
Hapiness: Humanist
import/export ratios: Industrialist
reducing global import: Benefactor
few buildings: Minimalist
