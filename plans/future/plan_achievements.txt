To give the player a sense of progress, they can reach achievements which then unlock additional game content.
Content:
- building types
- biomes

Ideas for achievements:
- 80% happiness (then 90%, 100%)
- 80-20 export-import ratio
- reducing a global import by 10% (20%, 30%, ...)
- reaching a certain number of exports (or imports, or hapiness) with a very few buildings constructed
- in forest biomes, keeping ecosystem damage to a minimum (20%, 10%, 5%, 0%) while having imports & exports

Evidently they are grouped. Each group can have sub-levels:
1. Novice
2. Expert
3. Master

For example, the ecosystem damage achievements can be:
- Novice Ecologist
- Expert Ecologist
- Master Ecologist

Hapiness: Humanist
import/export ratios: Industrialist
reducing global import: Benefactor
few buildings: Minimalist
