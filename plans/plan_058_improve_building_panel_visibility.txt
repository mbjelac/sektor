A "function panel" is a panel which shows when the player selects a building *from the building toolbar*.
It's purpose is to let the player know how this building functions

A "building panel" is a panel which shows when the payer has just constructed or selected an already constructed building on the map.
It's purpose is to let the player know what is the current state of the building.
